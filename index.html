Media intelligence · applied work

Turning a weekly editorial relay into a reusable intelligence workflow.

For Tosin Taiwo's Africa on Monday, the team translated a distinctive editorial identity into a practical AI-assisted thumbnail and title system—while protecting the judgment that made the work his.

The friction

A weekly four-round relay moved from script to AI to designer and back again, with Tosin carrying the creative direction and quality burden each time.

The intelligence

The team analyzed all 24 published thumbnails and identified the repeatable visual thesis: a contradiction between two opposing economic or social realities.

What Quoxient delivered

A six-page client kit and two prompts: one to generate and rank title-thumbnail concepts from a script, and one to render against the established visual language.

What was demonstrated

Two new topics produced usable first-pass concepts while preserving clear boundaries: the system supported the designer and editorial judgment; it did not claim to replace either or predict performance.

Enterprise decision intelligence

Turning a complex advanced-manufacturing initiative into a decision-ready operating environment.

Capital, customer, stakeholder, technology, and operating workstreams were moving simultaneously without one controlled view of what was current or decision-ready.

What Quoxient did

Reconciled the source and decision record, developed leadership briefs and audience-specific materials, structured the interdependent workstreams, and used AI for research, stakeholder profiling, meeting analysis, scenario development, and opportunity mapping.

Result

A controlled operating environment that gave leadership a current decision record, coordinated workstreams, and clearer gates for what could move internally or externally.

Executive AI enablement

Building an AI operating system around the leader who would use it.

An experienced advisor needed more than isolated automations. Positioning, business development, relationships, research, communication, and recurring execution had to reinforce one another inside one usable environment.

What Quoxient did

Combined transcript intelligence, behavioral analysis, strategic synthesis, and personalized workflows for research, investment memos, counterparty briefs, content, and weekly decision-making.

Result

An approximately 90-file business and AI operating system organized around the leader's real cognition, constraints, relationships, and work—with tools selected so the capability could be operated independently.
